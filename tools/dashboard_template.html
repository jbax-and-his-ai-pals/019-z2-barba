Zelda II: Barba Edition

NES 2.0 Mapper 176 (Dev) 5-Subgame Arcade

Play as Barba, the Palace 6 dragon, across Zelda II: The Adventure of Link. Hyrule's map, palace order, and item gates remain familiar, while 24×48 dragon movement, five-shot diagonal fire breath, dragon spells, fleeing/burning townsfolk, animated scenery, and a playable crossover arcade transform how you explore.

Dragon Combat & Spells Five-shot fire breath volleys, upward breath, rising head-butt, downstab stomp, Wyrmling flight, SCALES, INFERNO, and METEOR.
Living World & Towns Villagers flee, hide, or turn to ash; signs and buildings scorch; clouds drift, grass sways, and water/lava ripple via MMC3 scanline IRQs.
Crossover Subgame Arcade Discover and play 5 Barba-fied NES classics from Hyrule portals & Rauru's Arcade — or as standalone -compat MMC3 ROMs.
