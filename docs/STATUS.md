# Barba Edition status

The v1.05 tag marks the earlier MMC1 release. The development build uses
NES 2.0 Mapper 176 (FK23C), 512 KiB PRG, 512 KiB CHR, and 32 KiB banked WRAM.
It combines Barba Edition with the experimental *Where's Barba?* crossover
inside the same ROM. The portal is in Rauru. A wavy wipe leads to the crossover
title screen, and either Start + Select or the Moon ending returns Barba to
the same position and scroll in town. The older Mesen two-ROM prototype remains
available. [Crossover playtest instructions](TEST-PACK.md#experimental-waldo-portal)

The combined BPS includes *Where's Waldo?* game data as patch literals.
Public patch packaging is open. [Build inputs and outputs](BUILD.md)

For session-by-session history, see [docs/archive/CHANGELOG.md](archive/CHANGELOG.md). For the resolved November 15 audit, see [AUDIT-2026-11-15.md](AUDIT-2026-11-15.md).

## Changes since v1.05

- **Audio and presentation.** Sampled Barba voices, item and victory roars
  with raised-head poses, a Game Over Link portrait with five reworked laughs,
  a BREATH level-up label and icon, and updated death and ending animations.
- **Combat and rendering.** Carock's health and reflected-magic damage were
  rebalanced. Wizzrobe handling, Gooma's weapon visibility, and crowded-town
  shouts and combo counters were revised. The Darunia encounter fix keeps
  stomps from killing the invisible bubble generator and halting the game.
  Palace Ironknuckles can now bank downward breath back toward another
  enemy. Mesen verifies a rebound kill on a Bot. Try Busy Areas F4 to see
  the arc off an Ironknuckle's shield.
- **Scenery and towns.** The scanline IRQ drives the HUD split, parallax, and
  heat haze. Animated grass, water, lava, candle flames, and Saria quicksand
  give familiar locations more movement. Rauru's sage, Error, and Saria's
  mirror handoff have new interactions.
- **Crossover and testing.** Rauru can lead to a playable *Where's Barba?*
  side game in the combined development build. The checkpoint library now
  covers busy rooms, item and gem celebrations, movement hazards, and both
  crossover return paths.
