## 1. P1 — A lethal Inferno cast can softlock Thunderbird's arena

**Location:** `src/barba.asm:302`, `BarbaInferno`'s native Thunder call.

Inferno applies its immediate Thunder damage before the enemy update. If exposed
Thunderbird has 1–2 HP, this runs generic enemy death. The boss leaves active
state before its own update can install the special death state, so it disappears
while `$0728` remains 1. The player cannot leave the room.

The probe enters actual Great Palace room `$35`, waits until Thunderbird is on
screen and its intro has finished, and casts Inferno to expose it normally
(192 -> 190 HP). It then simulates a late-fight 2-HP state, reopens/closes the
spell menu through Start input, and casts again. Magic is **not refilled** between
casts: 255 -> 127 -> 0. The native casting routine permits the second 128-cost
cast from 127, accommodating the meter's 255-unit maximum. Such low boss HP is
reachable at lower attack levels (attack 1's 2 damage, or attack 5 leaving 1 HP).
The probe controls late boss HP and refills player health; it is not a complete
controller-only boss fight.

At the second cast, generic death changes the boss to state 2, then removes it.
The boss-death marker `$6E3F` remains 0, the scroll lock stays 1, and 400 frames
of right/jump input leave the player blocked at X490 in room `$35`.

This path comes from the retained native Thunder effect; it is a confirmed issue
in the current hack, not a proven newly introduced regression. The appropriate
fix must preserve Thunderbird's boss-specific death handling for spell kills,
as well as the existing projectile-kill path.

**Evidence:** `build/audit_thunderbird_inferno.lua` and
`build/audit_thunderbird_inferno.txt`. Run:
`python tools/emulator_test.py build/audit_thunderbird_inferno.lua`.

**Location:** `src/breath.inc:540-555`, `MeteorLand` / `MeteorEmit`.

| Same fixture and inputs | Idle lag / 300 | Active lag / 900 | Maximum live shots |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| METEOR enabled | 0 | **160** | 3 |
| METEOR disabled; ordinary breath control | 0 | **0** | 1 |

The METEOR run has 220 sampled frames with more than two live shots. Its NMI

This is a synthetic crowded-combat test, not a claim that every normal room has
this slowdown. The existing light-room `perf_meteor.lua` remains clean: 0/900 lag,
20,745 peak, 10 slams and 20 waves.

Budget shockwave allocation against enemy/projectile load, then verify crowded
combat as well as the existing standard fixture.

**Evidence:** `build/audit_perf_meteor_six.lua`,

## 7. P3 — Town scorch duplicate detection aliases different world pages

**Location:** `src/blocks.inc:226-234`.

Duplicate detection compares the nametable high/low address without
`SCORCH_PAGE`. Pages 0/2 (and 1/3) reuse those addresses. Burning a matching local
tile two screens away while the earlier 10-second fire is active is rejected as
already burning, suppressing both the new fire and its combo point. The renderer
correctly tracks pages, so the old offscreen fire does not compensate for this.

A controlled page-0 burn creates one entry and queues 10 bytes. The matching
page-2 call leaves only the page-0 entry, queues zero bytes and adds no point.
Compare `SCORCH_PAGE,x` with the candidate world page before accepting a duplicate.

**Evidence:** `build/town_audit_scorch_alias.lua` /
`build/town-audit-scorch-alias.txt`.

Make the display update conditional on a successful logical burn while preserving
Hidden Kasuto's separate reveal behavior. No WRAM overflow was reproduced; the

**Evidence:** `build/town_audit_forest_refused.lua` /
