## Bottom line

The game is stable. Random-input play across all 76 checkpoints ran 2.19 million frames, about 10 hours of game time,
with no crashes, no BRK and no execution from unmapped space. The only RAM code that ran was in page `$03`, which holds
the portal trampoline. The five-shot volley and combat checks pass once their stale test key is fixed. The biggest
problems are around the game rather than in its core:

1. The Donkey Kong portal takes over the Rauru spell house door at the natural standing position.
2. The performance tests stopped measuring cycles, so headroom is invisible. They still count lag frames.
3. The customizer's Build path fails for every non-vanilla configuration.
4. The default patch contains the other games' data, and the shareable no-portal default does not build.
5. Two tests that passed this afternoon now fail: `town_bush_sway` and `waldo_cave_facing`. Check these against your
   in-progress work first.

## 1. Bugs, ranked

### P1. The Donkey Kong portal takes over the Rauru spell house

The portal check in [`src/waldo_portal.inc:473`](../src/waldo_portal.inc) only tests town, room, page and X. It does
not check what the door is. [`tools/portal_pool.py:24`](../tools/portal_pool.py) describes these as safe flavor doors,
but five of the seven active portals sit on closed "opener" doors:

| Portal | Door | What it really is | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Donkey Kong | Rauru room `$01`, page 2 | **Spell house (SCALES)** | A/B tested |
| Super Mario Bros. | Ruto room `$05`, page 1 | Magic-refill lady (`$18`) | Service still works with her door open, tested at X 402 |
| Duck Hunt | Ruto room `$05`, page 2 | Healer (`$17`) | Service still works with her door open, tested at X 591 |
| Tetris | Mido room `$09`, page 1 | Closed door | No opener on a daytime walk; quest use unverified |
| Mega Man 2 | Mido room `$09`, page 2 | Closed door | Same as Tetris |
| Waldo | Rauru room `$01`, page 1 | Walk-in house, room `$1C`, one NPC | Entered with portals off |
| Friday the 13th | Ruto room `$05`, page 0 | Walk-in house, room `$1D`, one NPC | Entered with portals off |

At the Rauru door (world X 544), pressing Up at X 538, 541 or 545 boots Donkey Kong. Aiming about 7 px or more to the
left still enters the house and teaches SCALES, so this is **not a hard block**, but the natural position gets the
wrong result. Moving only the DK portal makes `tests/spell_door.lua` pass.

In Ruto, the healer and magic entries beat the portal while the door is open. With the door closed, the portal code
still fires on Up, so those doors do double duty. Decide whether that is what you want.

**Fix:** move DK off the spell house now. The better long-term answer is one arcade house (section 5). Add a test that
flags any portal on a `$8B` door, so sharing a door is always a deliberate choice.

### P2. Performance probes cannot see cycle counts

`tests/busy_rooms.lua:57,69` and all four `perf_*` scenarios hook `$C1B5` or `$C1A3`. The NMI now returns at `$C1B2`,
so they report `nmiMax=0`. Cycle headroom is invisible, although lag frames are still counted. `STATUS.md` and
`CONDUCTOR.md` still quote lower Old Kasuto lag (0, 13 and 30 frames in older runs), and the corrected probe measures
37 per 400. **Fix:** export a label for the NMI return and hook that.

### P3. Customizer builds fail, and its manifest would overstate what was applied

- [`tools/fe_catalog.py:536`](../tools/fe_catalog.py) passes `--output`, and `:540` passes `--no-waldo`. `build.py` has
  neither, so every non-vanilla prototype exits with code 2.
- With those fixed, only three settings reach the build. The manifest would still record the rest as applied, so
  `chaos` would claim shuffled items in a ROM with none, and turning Barba off would still produce Barba.

### P4. The shareable configuration does not build

All eight combinations of sampled audio, portals and boss bar were built. Seven build and boot. **Sampled audio + no
portals + Souls bar fails** with overlapping patches at `$FC90` (Ganon laugh data versus the Souls bar IRQ split).

This matters because of distribution. The default BPS is **2,957 KB** because it carries the other games' data, and
`docs/BUILD.md` acknowledges this for Waldo. The no-portal patch is **178 KB**. That is fine for personal builds, since
`build/` is git-ignored, but publishing the default patch would redistribute those games.

### P5. Smaller confirmed issues

- **Dialogue can re-trigger.** Pressing B every 16 frames keeps the healer's dialogue re-opening, so it never finishes.
  Vanilla closes normally at that cadence, and 6, 10 and 24-frame cadences work on Barba.
- **Lag in four rooms** (section 3).
- **Possible phantom Up on real hardware (from reading code, not reproduced).** `PollUpKey2` reads `$4016` directly
  instead of using the project's DMC-safe controller cache. A sampled roar could corrupt that read near a portal door.

## 3. Performance, with the probe corrected

Budget is about 29,700 NMI cycles. Of the 67 checkpoints measured, these exceed it (400 scripted frames each):

| Room | Lag frames | Peak cycles | HUD glitches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old Kasuto | 37 | 33,004 | 4 |
| Palace 2 crowded floor | 14 | 33,571 | 4 |
| Palace 5 Gooma approach | 10 | 31,843 | 0 |
| Darunia | 1 | 31,537 | 0 |

Sprite flicker is the other pressure point. The Dark Link room exceeds 8 sprites per line on 370 of 400 frames, and
the Gooma approach on 355, peaking at 18 on one line.

**ROM space is plentiful, with a caveat.** 165 of 256 PRG banks (8 KB) and 570 of 1,024 CHR banks (1 KB) are blank, but
much of that sits inside the subgames' outer blocks. Banks 16-29 (112 KB) sit directly after Zelda's code. Confirm what
Zelda's banking can reach before planning around it. The tighter limits are CPU time per frame and the nearly full hook
banks, so new systems should live in far-called banks.

## 4. Vanilla quirks and opportunities

- **Door entry is picky about position.** At the Rauru healer door, Up worked from 4 to 14 px left of the door tile and
  failed from 2 px right of it, on both vanilla and Barba. A wider, centered window would suit Barba's body.
- **SAVE ends in a dead-end screen (seen in the soak).** The "hold reset" caution screen spins forever. Returning to
  the title after saving is a cheap quality-of-life win.
- **Good customizer knobs (from general Zelda II knowledge, not tested here):** lives, XP loss on death, continue
  location, encounter rate, text speed, and Great Palace requirements.

## 5. Feature critique and new ideas (opinion)

**Critique**

- **Portals are the best surprise and the weakest integration.** Nothing in the world signals them, and they compete
  with real doors.
- **Town chaos has no consequences yet.** Villagers flee and signs burn, but every service keeps working. This is the
  biggest untapped system in the game.
- **Fireball range:** instead of raising the live-shot cap, try recycling the oldest shot when the cap is hit. Longer
  range would then stop starving fire rate, which is what broke Rebonack.
- **METEOR** still has Fire's cost and unlimited slams. A per-slam cost or slam cap would make it a decision.

**New ideas, roughly by value for effort**

| Idea | What it adds | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Barba's Arcade** | One house with cabinets for all subgames. Fixes P1, adds discoverability | M |
| **Infamy** | Burns and kills raise a meter. Towns lock doors, prices shift, guards appear, endings change | M |
| **Eat villagers** | The text already begs "DON'T EAT ME." Heals Barba, raises Infamy | S |
| **Arcade trophies** | Extend the Waldo hat reward template to every subgame | S each |
| **The Hoard** | XP drops become treasure, spendable on bribes and healing | M |
| **Roosts** | Nest checkpoints replace the long walk back after Game Over | M |
| **Breath types** | Spell-swapped fire, ember or scald with different arcs and block interactions | L |
| **Glide** | Hold A to glide. Needs logic rules because it bypasses gaps | M |
| **Boss Rush / New Game+** | Reuses existing boss setups | S to M |
| **Multiple endings** | Roast, spare or marry Zelda based on Infamy | S |

## 6. Customizer: what is really coupled

From the assembly call graph and the 184 labeled ROM patches:

| Tier | Contents | Separable? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Platform** | MMC3, NMI/IRQ, OAM shadow, far calls | Always on |
| **Barba kernel** | `barba`, `blocks`, `breath`, `encounter_trail`, `extra_fire`, `shield_rebound`, `shouts`, `tall_player` call each other in a cycle | One switch only |
| **Town cluster** | `town`, `sign`, `sage_basement` (also a cycle), plus `combo` and dialogue | Yes, after untangling `shouts` |
| **Leaf modules** | `boss_meter`, `souls_boss_bar`, `dark_barba`, `death_display`, `dialog_box`, `ending_fire`, `geldarm`, `ow_sparkle`, `sampled_roar`, `stomp`, `title_lava` have no assembly callers | Yes, by skipping their hooks |
| **Data patches** | Title art, palettes, text timing, boss stats, ending text | Easiest |
| **Wired today** | `SAMPLED_ROAR`, `WALDO_PORTAL`, `SOULS_BOSS_METER` | 7 of 8 combinations |

**Accidental couplings to cut first:**

- SCALES ticks from the `shouts` code, so removing town chaos would also break SCALES.
- `town_smoke` is called from the player renderer.
- `scanline_irq` calls `palace_roar` and `waldo_portal` directly.
- The catalog's `barba_lite` preset assumes town chaos can be turned off, which the code cannot do yet.

**The three decisions that shape everything**

1. **Barba-only or Link-or-Barba?** Barba-only is far cheaper. Link mode requires splitting the platform from the kernel.
2. **Personal or public?** Public means no embedded games in the patch. The clean route is a browser patcher where
   players supply their own ROMs, which never leave their machine.
3. **How do flags compose?** Pre-built patches need each feature at a fixed address in its own bank. Otherwise every
   combination needs a real assembler run.

**Suggested build order**

1. Fix the CLI mismatch. Generate each flag's status from what `build.py` can really do, and record only applied flags.
2. Tag every ROM patch with a feature group, and build the full flag matrix in CI.
3. Ship data and leaf-module flags.
4. Make portal placement door-safe.
5. Add a seed and logic solver, starting with item and spell shuffle. Barba needs his own logic rules, such as METEOR
   having to satisfy every Thunder check.
6. Add win conditions.

**New win conditions the flag system could offer**

- Classic crystals, or any N of 6
- **Burn the Kingdom:** torch every town
- **Arcade Champion:** beat N subgames
- **The Hoard:** reach a treasure target
- **Pacifist Dragon:** win with no villagers harmed
- **Kill Link:** Lava Link becomes the final boss
- Boss Rush and timed seeds
